I'm a Visual Designer evolving into the Interaction & Experience Design space. Two years of real industry work - Interaction Design at The Minimalist, packaging and brand identity at Out And Out, visual communications at The Souled Store taught me what design looks like under commercial pressure. My MS at the University of Limerick is where I'm formalising that instinct with research methods, AR/VR prototyping and user-centred frameworks.

What drives me is the space where visual craft meets human behaviour, designing things that don't just look right, but work right for the people using them.

Quick answers
I work across two things that don't usually sit together — UX research and visual design. On the research side, I dig into user problems, map out interactions and design the system that actually make sense for the people using them. On the visual side, I have a background in brand identity, graphic design and print media which have uplifted my UI skillset. The combination is what makes the work interesting. I don't just make things look good and I don't just make things that function, try to make things that do both.
I work in weekly cycles with a defined scope at the start of each week. Beginning with spending time to understand the problem and target audience, what are there motivation, goals to achieve and paintpoints throughout the journey. That thinking shapes every decision that comes after it. For tools: Figma Make and Cluade Design for UI and prototyping. Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and InDesign for brand strategy and system design. Blender and Unity for spatial and 3D exploration. Moreover, I understand HTML and CSS used to communicate with developers in their language and catch implementation issues before they ship.
Currently I am available for both full time roles and freelance or contract work. I'm currently completing my MS in Interaction and Experience Design at the University of Limerick and actively looking for opportunities in Ireland (Dublin, Limerick, Cork, Galway), India (Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru), or remote. Where I can contribute from day one while I finish the programme. If you're building a design team, working on a product that needs someone who thinks about both the research and the craft or have a brand or UI project that needs a fresh perspective, I'd like to hear about it.
Most designers come from one direction either visual designers who learned a bit of UX, or UX researchers whose wireframes need someone else to make them look credible. I bridge both, which means fewer handoffs, fewer mismatches between intent and execution and a designer who can own a project from the first user interview to the final high-fidelity screen. Moreover, genuinely engaged with how AI is changing the designer's role as a tool that accelerates ideation and frees up time for the thinking that actually matters. I use it in my workflow, understand its limits and can bring that awareness to a team.
Figma is my primary tool for UI and prototyping. Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign for visual and brand work. Premiere Pro for motion. FreeCAD and Unity for exploratory work in my MS. HTML/CSS and beginner React for front-end collaboration.
