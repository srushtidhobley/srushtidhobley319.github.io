About Me

Srushti Dhobley

AI isn't replacing designers. It's raising the bar for what designers need to understand. I'm a Visual Designer evolving into the Interaction & Experience Design space. Two years of real industry work - Interaction Design at The Minimalist, packaging and brand identity at Out And Out, visual communications at The Souled Store taught me what design looks like under commercial pressure. My MS at the University of Limerick is where I'm formalising that instinct with research methods, AR/VR prototyping and user-centred frameworks. What drives me is the space where visual craft meets human behaviour, designing things that don't just look right, but work right for the people using them.

I'm actively looking for Visual Designer, UI/UX Designer or Interaction Designer roles from September 2026 in Ireland, India (Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru) or remote.
